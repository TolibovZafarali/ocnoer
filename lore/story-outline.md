# Ocnoer — Story Outline

> This file is a high-level plot outline for lore and chapter planning.
> It is not final scene text and may change as the story is written in full.

## Act 1 — Funeral, Proposal, and Conspiracy

- King Maedric dies in Valestria.
- Vaelan becomes king.
- Foreign delegations arrive for the funeral.
- Prince Lucair asks for Ocnoer’s hand in marriage.
- Zareen gains influence at court.
- Ocnoer overhears a suspicious private conversation that hints at a plot against Vaelan.
- The court begins preparing for the journey to Mirath.

## Act 2 — Journey, Hollowpine, and Alvyn

- Ocnoer travels with Queen Maelis and King Vaelan toward Mirath.
- Tension grows during the journey.
- In Hollowpine, Ocnoer runs into the woods and meets Alvyn Rivers.
- The massacre happens while they are apart from the main party.
- Queen Maelis and King Vaelan are killed.
- Ocnoer realizes she may no longer be safe in Valestria.
- Alvyn promises to protect her.

## Act 3 — The Road to Mirath

- Ocnoer and Alvyn travel together toward Mirath.
- Their bond deepens through danger, travel, and shared vulnerability.
- They survive an attack on a Mirathi village.
- Alvyn proves himself brave and capable.
- They finally reach Mirath.

## Act 4 — Mirath, War, and Lucair’s Rise

- Ocnoer enters Mirath under political pressure and emotional conflict.
- King Aedric uses recent events as cause for war.
- Mirath moves against Valestria and Azdara.
- Lucair rises in power.
- Lucair kills Aedric and becomes king.
- He ends the war but begins abusing Ocnoer.

## Act 5 — Escape and Loss

- Ocnoer suffers under Lucair’s rule.
- Princess Nyra secretly helps Ocnoer by maintaining contact with Alvyn.
- Alvyn prepares an escape.
- Ocnoer flees Mirath.
- Nyra is killed by Lucair during or after the escape.

## Act 6 — Naito and the Ending

- Ocnoer and Alvyn flee east beyond the mountains toward Naito.
- Ocnoer is struck by a poisoned arrow during the escape.
- Her condition worsens over time.
- Alvyn refuses to leave her or surrender hope.
- In Naito, healers declare that she cannot be saved.
- Ocnoer and Alvyn share a final intimate scene.
- They die with the promise that they may find each other in another life.

## Notes for Project Context

- This file is for chapter planning and canon alignment.
- It should help Codex understand the major acts of the story.
- Final chapters, scenes, and dialogue may expand or change these beats.
