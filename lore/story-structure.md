# Story Structure

The Ocnoer project is structured like a visual novel.

Story hierarchy:

Chapter
→ Scene
→ Dialogue

Each scene contains:
- background image
- background music
- dialogue
- narrator text
- character portraits

Character placement rules:

- Ocnoer appears on the LEFT when she speaks or thinks.
- Other characters appear on the RIGHT.
- Narrator text appears in the CENTER.

Interaction is minimal.

The player only types responses during conversations with Alvyn Rivers.

These responses are stored and visible in the admin panel.
