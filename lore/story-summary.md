# Ocnoer — Story Summary

Ocnoer is a tragic romantic fantasy story set in the world of Thaloraz.

The story begins in Valestria after the death of King Maedric Valamere. His daughter, Princess Ocnoer, attends the funeral where Prince Lucair Velyr of Mirath arrives and asks for her hand in marriage.

The Mirathi delegation leaves Valestria first. Shortly afterward, Ocnoer travels toward Mirath with her mother Queen Maelis and her uncle Prince Vaelan to attend a knight's tourney and prepare for the wedding.

While passing through Hollowpine, they are attacked. Queen Maelis and Prince Vaelan are killed. The attackers appear to be Hollowpine men but are secretly agents from Azdara.

Ocnoer fears returning to Valestria if the attack was orchestrated by Princess Zareen. Before the massacre, Ocnoer meets Alvyn Rivers, who later promises to protect her.

Alvyn escorts Ocnoer to Mirath. During the journey they develop strong feelings for each other.

Once Ocnoer arrives in Mirath, King Aedric Velyr uses the situation as a reason to begin a war against both Valestria and Azdara.

Prince Lucair eventually kills his father and becomes King of Mirath. He ends the war, partly because of his feelings for Zareen, but begins abusing Ocnoer.

Lucair's sister, Princess Nyra, secretly helps Ocnoer escape by keeping contact with Alvyn Rivers. During the escape from Mirath, Nyra is discovered and killed by her brother.

Alvyn and Ocnoer flee east toward the mountains north of Mirath, heading for the distant land of Naito.

During the escape Ocnoer is struck by a poisoned arrow that slowly weakens her. Alvyn refuses to give up and takes her to Naito in hopes of finding a cure.

In Naito the healers reveal that she cannot be saved.

In the final scene, Alvyn lies beside Ocnoer as she dies. They speak about death and fate, and Alvyn promises they will meet again in another life.

The story ends with a subtle suggestion that this other life may be the present world.
