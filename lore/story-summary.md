# Ocnoer — Story Summary

Ocnoer is a tragic romantic fantasy story set in the world of Thaloraz.

The story begins in Valestria after the death of King Maedric Valamere. Because Maedric leaves no son, his brother Vaelan becomes king. During the funeral, Prince Lucair Velyr of Mirath arrives and asks for Princess Ocnoer’s hand in marriage.

As politics shift in Valestria, Queen Zareen gains greater influence. Ocnoer overhears a secret conversation that suggests Vaelan is being pushed toward danger, but she does not fully understand it.

The Mirathi delegation leaves first. Soon after, Ocnoer travels toward Mirath with her mother, Queen Maelis, and King Vaelan for a knight’s tourney and for preparations related to her marriage to Lucair.

While passing through Hollowpine, Ocnoer runs into the woods after an argument and meets Alvyn Rivers. She feels there is something familiar about him, though she cannot place it. While they speak, screams rise from Hollowpine. From a distance, they witness the massacre of Ocnoer’s party.

Queen Maelis and King Vaelan are killed. The attackers appear to be Hollowpine men, but they are secretly agents from Azdara.

After the massacre, Ocnoer believes she cannot safely return to Valestria. If Zareen was involved, going home may mean death. Alvyn promises to protect her and takes her with him to Mirath.

Their road to Mirath becomes the foundation of their relationship. The journey is dangerous and intimate, and they begin to develop strong feelings for one another. Along the way, they pass through a Mirathi village attacked by Azdaran forces, and Alvyn helps rally the villagers to survive.

Once Ocnoer arrives in Mirath, King Aedric Velyr uses the deaths in Valestria and the wider unrest as cause to begin war against both Valestria and Azdara.

Later, Prince Lucair kills his father and becomes King of Mirath. He stops the war, partly because of his feelings for Zareen, but begins abusing Ocnoer.

Lucair’s sister, Princess Nyra, secretly helps Ocnoer escape by keeping contact with Alvyn Rivers. During the escape from Mirath, Nyra is discovered and killed by her brother.

Ocnoer and Alvyn flee east toward the lands beyond the mountains north of Mirath, seeking refuge in Naito.

During their escape, Ocnoer is struck by a poisoned arrow. The poison slowly weakens her, but Alvyn refuses to give up and carries her onward in hope of finding treatment.

In Naito, they are told that Ocnoer cannot be saved.

In the final scene, Alvyn lies beside Ocnoer as she dies. They speak of death, fate, and love beyond one lifetime. Alvyn promises that they will meet again in another life.

The story ends with a subtle suggestion that this other life may be the one being lived now.
