# Ocnoer World Timeline

> This file is a lore/reference timeline for the project.
> It is not a complete chapter script and does not represent the full final story.
> Early events are more defined than later ones.
> Codex should use this file as narrative context, not as final implementation logic.

## Timeline Status

This timeline is currently most reliable from the death of King Maedric through Ocnoer and Alvyn reaching Mirath.
Later story beats may be expanded, changed, or reordered as the story is written in full.

## 1. Death of King Maedric

King Maedric Valamere of Valestria dies. Because he leaves no son, his brother, Prince Vaelan Valamere, becomes king.

Delegations from other kingdoms come to Valestria for the funeral:
- King Aedric Velyr and Prince Lucair Velyr of Mirath
- Prince Razin Saffryn of Azdara

During this time, Prince Lucair seeks Ocnoer’s hand in marriage.

Princess Zareen, Vaelan’s wife and now queen, also gains greater influence after Maedric’s death.

One night, while walking the corridor, Ocnoer overhears Zareen speaking privately with a man whose face she does not see. She does not recognize his voice. The man tells Zareen to send Vaelan to Mirath and let his old enemy deal with him.

The hidden meaning is that Vaelan is being sent toward danger. The enemy referenced is tied to Azdara.

## 2. Prince Lucair Proposes Marriage

Prince Lucair spends time with Ocnoer in the garden and presents Mirath as a future of power, promise, and queenship.

Afterward, Ocnoer hears discussion of the coming journey to Mirath for a knight’s tourney and for preparations related to her marriage.

Ocnoer also notices that Zareen does not want Vaelan to go, which confuses her because it clashes with what she overheard earlier.

When Ocnoer questions Vaelan privately and asks whether he has enemies, he denies it. He is proud, stubborn, and unwilling to appear weak.

## 3. The Royal Journey to Mirath

Ocnoer leaves for Mirath with Queen Maelis and King Vaelan.

The purpose of the journey is:
- the knight’s tourney in Mirath
- Ocnoer’s marriage to Prince Lucair

While passing through Hollowpine, Ocnoer argues with her mother and runs into the woods.

There she meets Alvyn Rivers.

Ocnoer feels she may have seen him before, but cannot place him. Alvyn denies knowing her, though in truth they met long ago when they were younger. Ocnoer does not remember it, but Alvyn does.

Alvyn is also on the road toward Mirath, seeking a path into knighthood.

As they speak, screams rise from Hollowpine.

Ocnoer tries to return, but Alvyn stops her because it is too dangerous. Together they witness the massacre from a distance.

Queen Maelis and King Vaelan are killed.

The attackers appear to be Hollowpine men, but in truth they are agents from Azdara.

## 4. Ocnoer and Alvyn Travel to Mirath

After the massacre, Ocnoer believes she cannot safely return to Valestria.

She fears that if Zareen was involved, then going home would mean death.

Her only remaining path is Mirath.

Alvyn promises to protect her and takes her with him.

Their road to Mirath becomes the foundation of their relationship. It is dangerous, intimate, and transformative for both of them.

During the journey, they pass through one of the Mirathi villages. The village is attacked by Azdaran forces.

At first the people believe they are doomed, but Alvyn rallies them and helps turn fear into resistance.

After surviving the attack, Ocnoer and Alvyn continue onward and finally reach Mirath.
