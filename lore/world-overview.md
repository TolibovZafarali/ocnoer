# World Overview — Thaloraz

The story takes place in the world of Thaloraz.

The main known kingdoms are:

Valestria
A western kingdom ruled by House Valamere.

Mirath
A powerful eastern kingdom ruled by House Velyr.

Azdara
A southern desert kingdom ruled by House Saffryn.

Aurelion
A north-western kingdom with strong noble houses and old alliances.

Frostgard
A distant frozen northern land ruled by warrior kings.

The story mainly takes place in Valestria, Mirath, and the lands between them.
